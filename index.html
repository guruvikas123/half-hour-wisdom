My story

My Journey & Philosophy

"Healing doesn't always begin with answers. It begins with having a safe space to be honest about what you feel."

My journey into mental health began at 13, when a psychiatrist visited my school and spoke about anxiety, depression, and the silent battles people fight. I went home moved, and spent hours reading about psychology and emotional pain. Somewhere in those pages, I knew I wanted to dedicate my life to helping people feel less alone.

My path led me to medical school. As a teenager and young adult living abroad, I faced my own share of loneliness, uncertainty, and personal challenges. Having personally navigated PTSD and suicidal ideation, I bring deep empathy and trauma-informed understanding into every session. I know what it feels like to survive emotionally difficult seasons while still trying to appear "okay."

My philosophy is simple: healing doesn't always begin with answers. It begins with having a safe space to be honest about what you feel. People are far more resilient than they realise — they often just need clarity, guidance, and someone willing to walk alongside them.

This is the space I hope to create: rooted in empathy, confidentiality, and genuine human connection. Helping people navigate relationships, self-worth, heartbreak, and personal growth is not just my profession. It is my purpose.
