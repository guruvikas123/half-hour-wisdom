About the show

One guest. Thirty minutes.
Everything worth knowing.

Half Hour Wisdom is a YouTube interview series that believes the most valuable conversations happen when you remove the formality and just talk. No teleprompters. No PR handlers. No rehearsed answers.

Every episode, a guest from a completely different world — a musician, a psychiatrist, a startup founder, a fitness coach — sits down for thirty unscripted minutes. We ask the professional questions. We ask the personal ones. And somewhere in between, the real conversation begins.

The format is simple: half an hour. Every time. Because wisdom doesn't need more time — it needs the right questions.

"You'd be surprised how much truth a person shares when they know the clock is ticking."

— The Half Hour Wisdom philosophy

Host & Creator

Vikas is the creator and host of Half Hour Wisdom. By day, he works in education — understanding what makes people learn, grow, and make decisions. By instinct, he's been curious about what drives people in every field imaginable.

Half Hour Wisdom was born from a simple frustration: most interviews are too long, too polished, and not personal enough. He wanted to sit across from a musician and a neuroscientist in the same week and ask them both the same question — "What does your field get completely wrong?"

The show is his answer. Thirty minutes. Unscripted. Always personal. Always worth your time.
